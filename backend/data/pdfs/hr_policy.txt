Kazi HR Policy Document

1. Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid leave per year. 
Sick leave requires a medical certificate if longer than 2 days.
Maternity leave is 3 months fully paid.

2. Payroll Policy
Salaries are paid on the 25th of every month.
Performance bonuses are reviewed annually in December.

3. Code of Conduct
Employees must maintain a professional demeanor at all times.
Remote work is allowed up to 3 days a week with manager approval.
